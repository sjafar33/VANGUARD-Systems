## Project name
Moonbase Academy

## High-level summary (draft)
Moonbase Academy is an offline iPhone and iPad game where children aged 7–12 run a tiny lunar training base. Four short drills cover power budgeting, life support, food production, radiation shielding and communication delay. Each drill asks the player to build a plan, predict the outcome, run a test, and learn from what happened. Real NASA data drives the results: crew oxygen and food rates from NASA's Life Support Baseline Values document, lettuce growth time from the Veggie experiment, the timing of an archived September 2017 solar particle event, and the Earth–Moon distance that sets the radio delay.
