## Project name
Moonbase Academy

## High-level summary (draft)
Moonbase Academy is an offline iPhone and iPad base-building game where children aged 7–12 build, repair, decorate and test their own lunar outpost. They earn Moon coins by learning, place buildings on a board, restore an old landing station, and then run **Test My Base**: a shadow test that uses the solar arrays and batteries they really built. When it fails, the game says why from the simulation itself, and the child goes back to improve the base or the plan. Four short drills cover power budgeting, life support, food production, radiation shielding and communication delay. Each drill asks the player to build a plan, predict the outcome, run a test, and learn from what happened. Real NASA data drives the results: crew oxygen and food rates from NASA's Life Support Baseline Values document, lettuce growth time from the Veggie experiment, the timing of an archived September 2017 solar particle event, and the Earth–Moon distance that sets the radio delay.
